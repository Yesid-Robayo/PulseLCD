# Changelog

**English** · [Español](CHANGELOG.es.md)

What each version of PulseLCD brought and fixed, newest first. Published versions have their files, their notes in English and
Spanish and their SHA256 in [Releases](https://github.com/Yesid-Robayo/PulseLCD/releases).

When a new version is published, it's added at the top with the same content as its release notes.

## 0.1.2

- **The Windows Fast Startup notice is gone.** It said Fast Startup made the display stay black at power-on, but turning it off
  didn't help: the display still went black. If you turned it off because of the notice, you can turn it back on.
- **The black screen at power-on is still being tested.** Its cause isn't known yet. The test machine now runs with Fast Startup on
  again to compare, and the FAQ in the README has what is known so far.

## 0.1.1

- **Frame your background image.** With an image as the background, the theme editor adds **Horizontal**, **Vertical** and **Zoom**
  under **Background**, so you choose which part of it is shown. You see the result as you move them, and the themes you already have
  stay centred.

## 0.1.0

First version. It's free, in beta and offered as is, **with no warranty**. It's **only for the Turing Smart Screen 3.5"** (480×320,
first revision); other sizes and models don't work.

### The display

- **Live mirror** of the panel in the window.
- **Metrics:** CPU, GPU, FPS with Frame Generation, RAM, disk, network, latency, uptime, clock and weather without an account.
- **Alerts** with a coloured frame and icons for temperature and peripheral batteries.
- **Pick what isn't a game** from the list of open programs, by the name of its window.
- **Screen cleaning,** by hand or on a schedule.

### Themes

- **Visual editor:** drag, resize, snap to guides, undo, fonts and your own images, with a preview drawn by the same code as the
  panel.
- **21 original themes:** space, pixel art, neon cities, dungeons, nature and more, with backgrounds generated by code.
- **Your library, your way:** edit any built-in theme directly, go back to the original, remove the ones you don't want, one by one or several at
  once, and pick which ones to restore without touching the rest.

### A panel that looks after itself

- **Resynchronises without resetting** at the start of every session.
- **Repaints by itself** after connecting and every 30 minutes.
- **Measures how long each frame takes** to detect a stuck panel and fix it.
- **Reconnects by itself** if the panel drops, and tells you to check the cable if it doesn't come back.
- **Clean shutdown:** it finishes the image in progress before the PC sleeps or shuts down.
- **Warns about Windows Fast Startup** when it's on, because it's linked to a black display at power-on, and shows the steps to turn it off.

### Light and safe

- **Runs without administrator rights.** Only a separate 350 KB helper has them, for CPU temperature and FPS.
- **No telemetry and no accounts.**
- **New-version notice,** off by default: it only asks GitHub for the version number, and downloads nothing.
